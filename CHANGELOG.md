## [Unreleased]

No version has been published yet: everything below will make up `0.1.0`.

No Man's Land left the pack on 2026-11-13 ([0019](docs/decisions/0019-leaving-no-mans-land.md)). - **The Hosts come on their own** (`fallow` 0.104.0, `hotes` module, [hotes](docs/plan/hotes.md)): at dusk, in a clearing where a player stands, one of the two peoples living around it hangs its mask on a post just outside the edge, and comes the next evening: its emissary stands in the post's place, never crossing the edge, with a woven offering mat one block inside. Talking to it shows three wishes drawn for the season (an item or any of a tag); a gift goes from the inventory onto the mat, a polite « Ne rien laisser » is allowed once a season. At dawn the emissary goes back into the mist and the mat holds a counter-gift, better as the bond grows (Inconnus, Voisins, Proches, Familiers, never shown as a gauge); forgetting rolls the mat up, the second time puts out the fires near the edge, the third keeps the people away for a season. The dialogue is now drawn like the pack's books: a loose sheet of their paper at the foot of the screen, written in their brown inks and rubric red, the portrait printed in it as a plate, ink pictograms for the answers (the question mark too), a wish's count written after its name; the window keeps one height, the portrait is drawn on the GUI's pixel grid, the five habitats behind the busts are redrawn in the Écorce's manner, the Braise mask has lava cracks instead of red bands, the Sables mask hanging leaves, and the six mask icons line up on one pixel. - **Attachment: caring for an item counts** (`fallow` 0.81.0, `eveil` module, [eveil](docs/plan/eveil.md)): each item grows attached to whoever takes care of it, said in one word in its tooltip and in the table's book: neglected, cared for, cherished. Cared for, an item worn out no longer forgets its last awakening: it stops and waits to be repaired. Cherished, it carries one more awakening. The metal now adds places too: steel and black steel one, diamond two, orichalcum three (five at most). Archie's Dinosaurs armours awaken from their own deeds (heat, heights, depths, dry roads, silence, approaching animals) and ask for a part of their beast besides the ink. - **Seventh pass on the additions since 2026-11-18** (`fallow` 0.94.0): Farmer's Delight's cutting board reads the tool in the main hand even when the click comes from the off hand, so a stopped tool no longer cuts that way. A stopped smithing hammer no longer turns stone or an anvil into a smithing anvil when you crouch, and no longer keeps Overgeared's smelters and blueprint workbench from answering: it is only refused where it would forge or convert.
- **Sixth pass on the additions since 2026-11-18** (`fallow` 0.93.0): a stopped smithing hammer in hand no longer keeps every door, chest and bed from answering (0.92.0 cut Overgeared's hammer listener everywhere, not only at its anvils). With a stopped tool in the main hand, a block that does nothing now leaves the click to the off hand (a torch is placed, a shield raised) as an empty hand would. Farmer's Delight's cutting board takes no stopped tool, whatever it is (axe, pickaxe, shovel). A spectator no longer opens doors with a stopped tool selected. The grey note shows only when the block did nothing. A called Host with nowhere to stand in front of you comes beside you, in a cave or over the void as under the Nether's roof, instead of going to the surface.
- **Fifth pass on the additions since 2026-11-18** (`fallow` 0.92.0): 0.91.0 refused every block that uses the item in hand while a stopped item was held, which also kept beds, chairs, cakes, composters, signs and Farmer's Delight's cooking pot from answering; now only the tools a block uses itself (shears, flint and steel, brush, knife, Overgeared's smithing hammer) are refused, and the block answers as to an empty hand (a door opens with stopped shears, a pumpkin stays whole). A stopped smithing hammer no longer starts Overgeared's forging, whose own listener runs before every other. Curses (Binding, Vanishing) stay on a stopped item. The gold warning comes back after an item that forgot or stopped is repaired, or once it is a tenth below the alert, and no longer sounds again and again while Mending holds it at the threshold. A called Host with nowhere to stand in front of you under the Nether's roof comes beside you.
- **Fourth pass on the additions since 2026-11-18** (`fallow` 0.91.0): a cared-for item stopped at its last point no longer works on any block that uses the item in hand without opening a window (this went too far and was narrowed in 0.92.0); its enchantments fall silent too (Protection, Thorns, Efficiency), except Mending, which repairs it; it no longer strikes a part of a large beast (the dragon, multipart dinosaurs), and breaks an armour stand again. Planks cut with a cared-for awakened axe in the crafting grid no longer destroy it at its last point: it stops there, as everywhere else. The gold warning comes back as soon as an item is repaired under the alert, as forgetting does (0.90.0 waited for a tenth more). The fleshing bench's station no longer plays a scrape for a stopped knife. Shaking leaves: a stick in hand always shakes, and empty-handed only food you can eat, a shield, a bow or another usable item in the off hand keeps the click. A called Host no longer stands at the bottom of a lake or under lava, and in the Nether it comes at your height.
- **Brumaire, black particles**: particles without a normal (falling leaves of Particular among them) were drawn as black shapes; they now face the eye and take the light of where they are.
- **Third pass on the additions since 2026-11-18** (`fallow` 0.90.0): a mended point in the red no longer costs an awakened item its attachment again and again (Mending used to take 12 points per orb, and every restart took them once more); the red counts once per passage, and only a repair a tenth below the alert lets the next passage count and warn again. A cared-for item stopped at its last point now serves no more at all: shears no longer shear a sheep, a pumpkin or a beehive, flint and steel no longer lights a creeper or TNT, the brush no longer brushes, the knife no longer scrapes the fleshing bench, and stopped armour no longer protects (it holds, empty, until repaired); a grey line says why each time. Hitting an item frame, a boat or a painting with it works again. Only a player's hands hold an item back: in a dispenser or a machine it wears out like any other. Shaking leaves no longer takes the click from a shield, food or a bow in the off hand. A Host summoned with data, or saved before masks were always worn, gets its mask back when loaded. A Host called by command stands at the player's height (in a cave, under the Nether's roof) instead of on the top of the world. A ferry knot tied too far from the first one is refused without loading that one's chunk. Adorable Hamster Pets no longer posts its guide warning in the chat (several lines in capitals pointing to a Discord server, with nothing in its configuration to turn it off); its guide book stays in EMI and can still be crafted.
- **Second pass on the additions since 2026-11-18** (`fallow` 0.89.0): a cared-for awakened item stopped at its last point now serves no more at all (no bow shot, shield, hoe, shovel, flint and steel or fishing rod), where 0.84.0 only stopped mining and striking; its wear is held back after the item's own damage hook, so a mod that raises it cannot break it; mended by a point at the edge it no longer repeats its note; an awakened item burnt or blown up on the ground now really takes its deeds with it (0.84.0 watched a method the game never calls there). An item that has lost its place can no longer be awakened from the table's book or the crouching window even for the second before its places are recounted. Two enchanting tables in range no longer fight over the mist and the muted world. At the forge, a blueprint is no longer offered to the stone anvil (it went into the grid), a piece in hand is only laid if it is still there when the camera arrives, and the recipe book counts the piece on the cursor. Antique Atlas has tiles for the three clearing biomes (it logged that it had none).
- **Additions since 2026-11-18 checked again** (`fallow` 0.84.0): an awakened item that is cared for now stops at its last point however fast it wears (a tool worn several points in one second could break and lose everything), and can no longer mine or strike until it is repaired; its tooltip at 90 % says it will keep its awakening instead of warning that it will forget it; an item that loses the place it was ready to fill (no longer cherished) is no longer ready, so it can never carry more awakenings than it is allowed; only a real repair (a tenth of its wear at least) grows attachment, not a point mended now and then; deployers and other machines holding a tool no longer count deeds; an awakened item burnt or destroyed on the ground takes its deeds with it. Adding a hide to a tanning vat that was already soaking restarts its timer for good (the step already planned fell too early, so the new hide came out under-tanned); a drying rack that the cold stops says so (« Trop froid : sans feu, il ne sèche pas. »). A ferry pulley on a ship built another way (Create, Sable) no longer ties it to the rope without the raft checks; the first knot of a rope waits five minutes, not forever; ropes are no longer taken when the second post is gone; switching the towing off during a fight lets the ship go. An emissary always wears its people's mask, also without Archie's Dinosaurs or when summoned by `/summon`, and its dialogue closes when it leaves; called while looking straight up or down it no longer lands on the player. A shy animal can no longer get its flight goal twice.
- **Motes and sand that follow the wind** (`fallow` 0.85.0, `vent` and `climat` modules): the game's particles bring any starting speed down to a few hundredths of a block a tick in a partly random direction, so the wind motes shown without shader and the sand of the storms hardly moved. They are now the pack's own grain (`vent.Mote`), which follows the local wind every tick, gusts included, fades in and out and goes out in a solid block; the motes also thin out and darken at night as meant (the sky brightness was read on the wrong scale), and the sand takes the light of the sky instead of turning grey. Without shader, a clearing in rain now opens about half as far as in fair weather, so the shower keeps its veil.
- **Second review of the game without shader** (`fallow` 0.87.0): the mist without shader told caves from the open with a heightmap the server never sends to the client, so it also acted underground; it now reads the sky light, and starts afresh after a cave, water, another world or a new connection. The cloud puffs of a clearing that opens or closes were born at the bottom of the world for the same reason and are now seen on the ground, under the trees too (`fallow` 0.88.0). Under a shader pack the storm sand takes the scene's light instead of glowing like a torch. Wind motes no longer rise in rain or over water. Brumaire's dust in torchlight underground leaves the shader, Particular's cave dust drawing it already.
- **Gestures and leaves checked again** (`fallow` 0.83.0): a fall into a canopy now keeps half the damage the same fall would do on the ground (`feuillage` module), where Passable Foliage's own cushion took almost all of it away (a 26-block fall into leaves did nothing); chickens, bats and other creatures that ignore falls still ignore them, Feather Falling still counts. Shaking leaves works with a stick in hand, so the first stick picked up no longer stops the gesture; a block or torch in the off hand is placed on leaves again instead of shaking them, Create's deployers and other fake players cannot shake leaves for sticks, and Regions Unexplored's apple leaves are picked again instead of shaken. A book on a lectern is read on the lectern again (Hands-On Vanilla): Scholar's lectern screen had taken it over (`replace_screens_on_lectern = false`); books in hand keep Scholar's two pages. Campfire smoke sent by another mod (Sooty Chimneys' chimneys) follows the wind without being drawn into the pack's campfire plumes or recoloured.
- **The enchanting table cannot be bypassed** (`fallow` 0.73.0, `eveil` module): with Hands-On Vanilla in the pack, the enchanting table could open vanilla's enchanting window instead of the awakening station, depending on load order, and let anyone enchant the old way. The table now always opens our station, and vanilla's enchanting window closes as soon as it opens.
- **Ships, ferry and towing checked again** (`fallow` 0.66.0): at the edge of the disc a boat or a horse was stopped dead at the start of the headwind and a player on foot lost half their speed at once, instead of being slowed little by little (the push is now applied by the player's own client); a ferry raft could be let go when its area loaded again; a raft touching a pier took the pier with it (now refused, « Le radeau touche la rive ou le fond »); ropes in the off hand were not counted; a swimming horse was towed by a hooked fish like a boat. After an independent review: a ferry post carried off by a Sable ship gave its ropes back twice; the speed of Sable ships was read half the time at half its value, so the wind, the ferry and the fish pushed too hard (the ferry is retuned to about 1 block/s in still water); a raft smaller than 3 blocks crossed the edge of the disc; a fish could tow an airship; the bobber ran away from a boat that lagged.
- A tanning vat, drying frame or fleshing beam pushed by a piston or carried by a Create contraption no longer drops its hides or leather while keeping them at its new place (a leather duplication) (`fallow` 0.35.2).
- The hearth rest no longer counts a player riding a moving boat or minecart as still; sitting on a seat still counts (`fallow` 0.35.2).
- Punchy!'s mixin is now skipped with a warning instead of crashing the game if an update renames its target (`fallow` 0.35.2).

- Fixes from the add-on audit (`fallow` 0.34.2): a clearing lantern, vigil fire or dissipator moved by a piston or a Create contraption, or placed by something other than a player, now opens its clearing where it stands instead of leaving the mist back; a tanning vat or drying frame moved the same way, or left while the tannery module was off, resumes instead of freezing; right-clicking a loaded fleshing beam with another raw hide no longer hands the first one back, and only an empty hand takes it back; emptying the vat with a bucket keeps the bark at the bottom; the EMI hiding lists no longer stop at an unknown id (Surface Samples' test sample, which does not exist, left Steam 'n' Rails' decorative tracks visible); the `xp` and `outils` switches moved to the world's `serverconfig/fallow-server.toml`, sent to every player, so a client can no longer disagree with the server and stop mining; the mixins into Archie's Dinosaurs, Distant Horizons' Chunky link and the Chorusoatl armour are skipped with a warning when an update removes what they target, instead of crashing the game.
