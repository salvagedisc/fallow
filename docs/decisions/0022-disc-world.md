# 0022 — A disc world with an edge

- **Date**: 2026-11-13
- **Status**: kept (the team's choice of 2026-11-13: radius 6,000 and a real edge); the edge seen in a test client on 2026-11-13 (on foot, by teleport, in a boat); the continent mapped by sampling on two seeds, not walked yet

## Context
Since [0019](0019-leaving-no-mans-land.md) the terrain is Tectonic with the Regions Unexplored biomes, and it has no end: continents and oceans follow one another for ever, a player can sail away from everything the pack places, and a server world grows without limit. The team wants a world in the manner of Drehmal: one land that can be known whole, with a size chosen on purpose, an edge that nobody crosses by sailing, and room kept for more land in later updates.

Drehmal is a painted map (12,000 blocks wide, about 4 GB, shipped outside Modrinth, years of work by a team). Modrinth caps a file at 500 MiB, and a painted map would freeze the terrain mods of the pack. The world has to stay generated.

## Options
| Criterion | A. Vanilla world border | B. Chunky Border | C. Computed disc and an edge in the `fallow` mod |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shape of the limit | Square | Round | Round |
| What is inside | Whatever the seed gives: maybe mostly sea | Same | One continent, whatever the seed |
| A boat or a mount at the limit | Stopped by the wall | The rider is taken off and sent back alone (`player.teleport`), the boat stays outside | Brought back with its rider |
| Look | A blue wall | A wall drawn by the client | Nothing is drawn: beyond the edge there is only open sea |
| Texts | None | English, in its own files | French, with the mod's texts |
| Cost | One command per world | One more mod (its 1.21.1 build dates from 2024-06) | A script and a module of ours |

## Decision
Option C.

**The disc** ([`scripts/terrain/disc.py`](../../scripts/terrain/disc.py), data written to `pack/kubejs/data/fallow/`). Two Lithostitched modifiers wrap two inputs of the terrain; Tectonic and the biome mods still generate everything from the seed.
- `tectonic:noise/raw_continents`: inside the disc a floor turns most of the sea of the seed into low land and a slow noise keeps a few inland seas; from about 550 blocks before the coast a cap brings the value down, to the coast at a mean radius of 5,700 and to deep sea beyond. Three noises make the coast wander by a few hundred blocks. The cap stops at -0.47: under -0.5 Tectonic switches to its island mode and makes land again.
- `minecraft:overworld/temperature`: the latitude replaces most of the seed's temperature. The north is cold and the south hot, with bent limits.

**The edge** (`bord` module of the `fallow` mod, 0.10.0, [details](../plan/mod-fallow.md#bord-module)). A circle of radius 6,000 around 0, 0, read from `data/fallow/monde/bord.json`, written by the same script. A player who passes it is brought back two blocks inside, with the boat or the mount; a message warns 64 blocks before. Spectators pass. Since 0.59.0 a headwind pushes back instead, ships included, and the teleport is only a safety net ([0046](0046-wind-on-ships.md)).

**Size.** The disc was measured by sampling the generator every 64 blocks, without generating a chunk (bench command, two seeds). Inside the edge, 63 to 73 km² are land, out of the 113 km² of the circle; the furthest land is about 5,800 blocks from the centre, so the coast stays about 200 blocks from the edge. From coast to coast the continent is about 11,400 blocks: some 45 minutes of walking in a straight line on flat ground, far more with the relief, the mist and no elytra. The test seed has 81 land biomes. Drehmal's map is 12,000 blocks wide: the two worlds have the same span. A radius of 6,000 is kept for the first world; more land comes by extension (below), not by a larger first disc that the pack could not fill with places worth the walk.

**Later extensions.** The radius and the shape are data. An update that adds land writes new shapes beyond the present edge in `disc.py` and moves the edge out. The sea between the edge and the view distance of the players has been generated by then: new land starts at least 600 blocks beyond the old edge, or the server owner trims that ring first (`chunky trim`), so that it is generated again.

## Consequences
- A world made before this decision keeps its chunks; new chunks follow the disc, with a seam between the two. Nobody has played yet: worlds are started again.
- The seed still decides everything inside the disc: relief, biomes, inland seas. Two seeds were mapped (`fallow`: 73 km² of land; `8675309`: 63 km²).
- Structures that only fit a climate or a terrain are counted again on the disc: the [monuments](../plan/monuments.md) census dates from before it.
- A server owner who pregenerates asks Chunky for a circle of radius 6,200 around 0, 0 (the edge plus the view distance): about 472,000 chunks. Measured on the full pack with four cores: about 13 chunks a second and 17 KB a chunk on land, so at most ten hours and 8 GB (sea chunks are lighter).
- The far landscape of Distant Horizons goes on beyond the edge: it is open sea.
- A ship of Create Aeronautics is not a mount: its pilot was not brought back, and the ship passed. Since [0046](0046-wind-on-ships.md) the headwind pushes the ship itself back.

## Rejected
- Option A: a square around a disc leaves four corners of open sea, and the wall of the game breaks the mist.
- Option B: read in its code (`BorderCheckTask`), it teleports the player alone to the last position inside. On a boat the rider ends in the water and the boat stays beyond the limit.
- A painted map: see Context.
- A fixed seed for everybody: not needed for the disc. It stays possible later (a server sets `level-seed`), if the team wants one shared world with known places.
